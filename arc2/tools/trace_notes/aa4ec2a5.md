## The task

The task, `aa4ec2a5` from the public evaluation set, gives three examples and one test input. Blue shapes sit on a yellow background. In each output, every shape gets a red outline one cell thick, corners included. A shape that encloses part of the background turns teal, and the enclosed cells turn fuchsia. Shapes that enclose nothing stay blue.
